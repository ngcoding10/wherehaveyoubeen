About & privacy

Your logbook is saved in this browser. If you sign in with Google, it's also saved to your own private account, which only you can read. Nothing is sold or shared, and there are no ads or trackers. Place names you type into Quick add that aren't in the built-in list are looked up on OpenStreetMap's public search service.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors via OpenFreeMap. Borders by Natural Earth. Cities by GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Built with MapLibre. Pixel art made for this site.
