WHERE HAVE YOU BEEN

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Where Have You Been is a free map logbook for the places you have visited. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your information, and the site currently shows no ads and does no tracking.

Who this covers

This policy covers Where Have You Been at ngcoding10.github.io/wherehaveyoubeen. "We" means Nico, the individual developer who runs it as a personal project.

What we collect

  • Your logbook: places you have visited, your wishlist, notes, dates, your home base, and your settings.
  • Signed out: all of this stays in your browser's local storage on your device. We never receive it.
  • Signed in: your logbook is also saved to your account, where only you can read it.
  • Account details: sign-in is optional and uses Google. We receive your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Friends (only if you sign in and use it): a username, your first name, your travel stats and your visited places.

The sign-in and one optional username are shared across the other apps I run (The Pantry, Where Have You Been and Movi). Using one app does not create a profile in another.

We don't collect payment details or contacts. The site currently shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Who can see what

Your logbook is private to you. If you use the friends feature, your username, first name, travel stats and visited places are visible only to friends who have accepted your request. Your notes, dates and home base are never shared with anyone.

Locate me

"Locate me" asks your browser for your location. The coordinates are used on your device to find the nearest town and are not sent to us or to any server. The map does move to where you are, so your browser loads map tiles for that area from OpenFreeMap, as it does for any area you look at. If you choose "Set home", a rounded version of that point is saved as your home base, in your browser and, if you are signed in, in your account.

Services we use

We don't sell or rent your information. These services handle data only to provide what the app needs:

  • Google Firebase (United States) provides sign-in with Google, stores signed-in data in Firestore, and runs Cloud Functions. Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise looks at device and browser signals to tell real visitors from bots. Google also serves the Firebase code library to your browser.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles. Your browser loads them directly.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives the place names you type into Quick add when they are not in the built-in list.
  • jsDelivr serves the country flag icons.
  • Google Fonts serves the app's fonts.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub may log IP addresses.

Your browser contacts these services directly, so they can see your IP address. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your cloud data while you want it kept. You can delete your Where Have You Been data from inside the app. Your logbook, friends profile and friend requests are then removed from your account. Your sign-in is removed when none of the apps has data left for you. A logbook saved in your browser stays there until you clear it.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Settings → Export logbook.
  • Delete your data: open your account, then choose "Delete my Where Have You Been data". This can't be undone.
  • Use it signed out: everything works without an account, and your logbook then stays on your device.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please keep a copy of anything you care about by exporting your logbook.

Children

This app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If ads or tracking are ever added, this page will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Open an issue at github.com/ngcoding10/wherehaveyoubeen/issues.
